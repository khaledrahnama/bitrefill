How it works

Three steps, zero clicks.

1

You describe the intent

"Send $15 to Alice in Lagos, Bob in Manila, and Carlos in Mexico City."

2

The agent reasons and routes

Local LLM picks the right instrument per country — airtime for Nigeria, Globe for Philippines, Telcel for Mexico. No hardcoded rules.

3

One invoice, delivered autonomously

Pays from balance via Bitrefill API. Polls orders[0].status until delivered. Returns redemption codes.
